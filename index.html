Services

Residential tile installation and repair, quoted per job after an on-site measure.

Kitchens & Backsplashes

Ceramic, porcelain, and natural stone backsplashes, counter surrounds, and full kitchen floors — laid out to the cabinet line, not to the box.

Bathrooms & Showers

Shower walls, pans, niches, and bath floors. Full waterproofing (Schluter-class membranes) under every wet-area install.

Floors & Patios

Large-format porcelain, wood-look plank tile, and exterior pavers. Level-prep, crack isolation, and expansion joints done right.

Repairs & Regrouting

Cracked or hollow tile, failed grout, loose pavers, and small punch-list work. Honest about when a repair is worth it — and when it is not.

Design & Layout

Pattern selection, layout dry-runs, and material take-offs. I'll mark the floor with you before a single tile is cut.

Free Estimates

On-site measure, written scope, and a fixed price. No deposit until the scope and schedule are agreed in writing.
